Process calculations & what is happening

Simplified model equations used: Q₁₂ = 0.45·V₁₂·√max(h₁−h₂,0); Qout = (0.12 + 0.28·Vout + disturbance)·√h₂; dh₁/dt = 0.65·pump − Q₁₂; dh₂/dt = Q₁₂ − Qout.

Operator panel

Learning corner

  1. Press Start.
  2. In Manual mode, change pump input to increase or decrease Tank 1 inflow.
  3. Change the inter-tank valve to influence Tank 2.
  4. Change outlet valve opening to alter draining.
  5. Switch to Automatic, adjust setpoint, and watch PID control.
  6. Try extra disturbance and compare the plot.
Tank balances: A₁ dh₁/dt = Qin − Q₁₂; A₂ dh₂/dt = Q₁₂ − Qout. The connecting flow is driven by the level difference. Automatic mode adjusts pump output to reduce Tank 2 level error.
